Finally Afslutningsvis Abschließend

The protocol responds —under your control. Protokollen reagerer —under din kontrol. Das Protokoll reagiert —unter Ihrer Kontrolle.

The treating psychologist, or more broadly the mental health professional using Deputy, keeps control over structure, sequence, and professional boundaries at every step. Deputy-protokoller er interaktive — kontrolleret interaktive. Selected, controlled AI components let the protocol respond to the person working through it, always inside the boundaries that professional has already set. Udvalgte, kontrollerede AI-komponenter gør det muligt for protokollen at reagere på den person, der arbejder sig igennem den — altid inden for de rammer, den fagperson allerede har fastsat.
